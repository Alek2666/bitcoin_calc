A Job at the Bookstore
MS. PARKER: Good morning, Omar. Please sit down.
OMAR: Good morning, Ms. Parker. Thank you for meeting me.
MS. PARKER: Is this your first job interview?
OMAR: Yes, it is, but I worked at my uncle's bookstore last summer.
MS. PARKER: Oh, that's good. What did you do there?
OMAR: I helped customers and put new books on the shelves.
MS. PARKER: Why do you want to work here?
OMAR: Well, I love books, and I like talking to people.
MS. PARKER: Can you work on Saturdays? We are very busy on weekends.
OMAR: Yes, I can. I'm free on Saturdays and Sundays.
MS. PARKER: Great! You can start next Monday at nine o'clock.
OMAR: Thank you very much! I'll be here on time.
