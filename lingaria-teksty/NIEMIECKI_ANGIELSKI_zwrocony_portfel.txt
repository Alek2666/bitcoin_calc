The Wallet in the Park
Priya was in a hurry on Tuesday morning because she was afraid of missing her bus.
She ran through the park, and somewhere on the way her wallet fell out of her coat pocket.
She didn't notice anything until she wanted to buy a coffee near her office.
"I can't believe it," she told the cashier after searching her bag for the third time.
All her cards, some cash, and a photo of her grandmother were in that wallet.
Priya felt sick, but she called her bank first and blocked the cards.
That afternoon, an elderly man named Mr. Alvarez found the wallet on a bench in the park.
He looked inside and found one of her business cards, so he phoned the company where she worked.
"I think I have something that belongs to one of your employees," he told the receptionist.
After work, Priya met him at the park, and he handed her the wallet with a smile.
Nothing was missing, not even the cash, so she offered him a reward.
He shook his head and said, "Someone helped me once in the same way, so now it's my turn."
