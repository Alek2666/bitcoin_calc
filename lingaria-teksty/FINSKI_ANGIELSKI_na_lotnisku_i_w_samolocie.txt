Flight to Boston
Last Friday, Maria flew from Denver to Boston to visit her sister.
She arrived at the airport two hours before her flight.
At check-in, the agent asked, "Do you have any bags?"
"Just one suitcase," Maria said, and the agent printed her boarding pass.
Then she went through security and walked to her gate.
Her seat was 14A, next to the window, and she was happy.
On the plane, the flight attendant asked, "Would you like chicken or pasta?"
Maria chose the pasta and a glass of orange juice.
After four hours, the plane landed in Boston.
Her sister was waiting at baggage claim with a big smile.
