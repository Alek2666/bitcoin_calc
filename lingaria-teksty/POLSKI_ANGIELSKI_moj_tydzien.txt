My Week as a Student
My name is Lina and I am a student.
I get up at seven o'clock every day.
I have breakfast and take the bus to class.
On Monday and Tuesday I have English class.
On Wednesday I study with my friend Jon.
We drink coffee and we talk a lot.
On Thursday I work at a small shop.
I finish work at six and go home.
In the evening I cook dinner and watch TV.
On Friday I meet my friends at a cafe.
On Saturday I clean my room and call Mom.
On Sunday I relax and read a book.
I like my week because every day is different.
